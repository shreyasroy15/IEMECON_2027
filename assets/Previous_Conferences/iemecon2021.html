IEMECON 2021

The IEEE International Conference on Internet of Everything, Microwave Engineering, Communication, and Networks (Virtual Edition)

December 1-2, 2021 | Virtual Event

Conference Overview

The 10th IEEE International Conference on Internet of Everything, Microwave Engineering, Communication, and Networks (IEMECON 2021), held as a Virtual Edition, demonstrated remarkable resilience during the pandemic. The conference was organized in Jaipur, India, on December 1st-2nd, 2021, and brought together thought-provoking keynotes and sessions on cutting-edge topics.

IEMECON 2021 aimed to bridge the gap between academia, industry, and government ethics, fostering meaningful dialogue among diverse stakeholders, including:

  • Scientists and Researchers
  • Engineers and Technical Professionals
  • Corporate Professionals
  • Scholars and Students

The conference featured focus areas including AI, Security, and Cyber-Physical Systems, addressing contemporary challenges and opportunities in the field.

Virtual Collaboration During Pandemic

IEMECON 2021 successfully adapted to the pandemic environment, maintaining the quality and impact of the conference through virtual collaboration, enabling global participation and knowledge exchange among the international research community.
