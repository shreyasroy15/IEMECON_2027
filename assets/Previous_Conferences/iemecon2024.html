IEMECON 2024

The IEEE International Conference on Internet of Everything, Microwave Engineering, Communication, and Networks

October 24-26, 2024 | Jaipur, India

Visit Official 2024 Website IEEE Xplore Proceedings

Conference Overview

The 12th IEEE International Conference on Internet of Everything, Microwave Engineering, Communication, and Networks (IEMECON 2024) took place at RIC Jaipur, India, from October 24th to 26th, 2024. This landmark event broke previous records with maximum participation from different institutions worldwide.

IEMECON 2024 featured:

  • Dynamic Sessions
  • International Keynotes
  • Invited Tutorials
  • Networking Opportunities
  • Global Participation

The conference brought together researchers, engineers, academics, and industry professionals to showcase innovations and collaborate on solutions for the future of embedded systems, microwave engineering, and communication networks.

Record-Breaking Success

IEMECON 2024 achieved unprecedented participation levels, establishing itself as one of the premier conferences in the field. The three-day event fostered meaningful collaborations and presented groundbreaking research across all technical tracks, making it a significant milestone in the conference's history.
