IEMECON 2019

The International Conference on Information Technology, Electromechanical Engineering, and Microelectronics

March 13-15, 2019 | Jaipur, India

Conference Overview

The International Conference on Information Technology, Electromechanical Engineering, and Microelectronics IEMECON 2019 took place in the vibrant city of Jaipur, India, from March 13th to 15th, 2019. This prestigious event served as a key platform for researchers, educators, and students to come together and engage in intellectual discussions, exchanging ideas on critical issues, emerging trends, and innovative developments within the fields of:

  • Information Technology
  • Electromechanical Engineering
  • Microelectronics

The conference fostered collaborative networking opportunities and provided a forum for the dissemination of cutting-edge research, enabling participants from academia and industry to share their knowledge and expertise with a global audience.

Conference Impact

IEMECON 2019 successfully brought together thought leaders, researchers, and innovators to present groundbreaking work and engage in meaningful discussions about the future of technology in these critical domains. The conference provided valuable insights into emerging trends and advanced research methodologies that continue to shape the industry today.

Access Conference Proceedings

The proceedings of IEMECON 2019 are now available through IEEE Xplore. Access peer-reviewed research papers, technical presentations, and keynote sessions from the conference.

The proceedings include all accepted papers and presentations from the conference, organized by technical tracks and session themes.
