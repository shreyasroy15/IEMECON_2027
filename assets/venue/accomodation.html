Accommodation

Find Your Perfect Stay in the Pink City

Welcome to Jaipur, the vibrant and culturally rich capital city of Rajasthan, India. Whether you’re here for a leisurely holiday or a business trip, finding the perfect accommodation is key to ensuring a delightful stay. Jaipur offers a plethora of options for every kind of traveler, from luxurious heritage hotels to cozy guesthouses and modern boutique hotels.

For Authors & Students

University Hostel

Accommodation is available at our University hostel for both boys and girls, subject to availability. This is an ideal option for student authors seeking a convenient and academic environment.

INR 400/- per day

(Includes Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner)

Booking Assistance +91-7980140443 +91-8107857478

General Assistance

Jaipur offers diverse options from heritage properties to modern business hotels. Our team is available to assist you with queries related to local guesthouses or hotel recommendations near the venue.

Note: Hostel rooms are limited — book early to avoid disappointment.

Important Notes

Complimentary Transport

Transportation will be provided by UEM Jaipur from both Jaipur Railway Station and Jaipur Airport for the convenience of participants.

Allocation Policy

Accommodation in the university hostel will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. Early booking is highly recommended.

Booking Details

Participants are requested to share travel dates and specific requirements. For group bookings, please coordinate explicitly with university authorities.
