Reach to Venue

Your Journey to UEM Jaipur Starts Here

Experience a smooth journey to the University of Engineering & Management (UEM), Jaipur. Our world-class campus is conveniently accessible from all major transport hubs.

From Jaipur Junction

Distance: Approximately 38 km
Travel Time: 50 - 60 minutes (via Sikar Rd/NH11)
Best Option: App-based cabs (Ola/Uber) or prepaid taxis are readily available outside the station.
Directions: Head north on Sikar Road (NH11). UEM Jaipur is located approximately 6 km past Chomu on the left side.
Get Detailed Directions

From Jaipur Airport (JAI)

Distance: Approximately 45 km
Travel Time: 1 hour 15 minutes
Best Option: Pre-paid taxi counter inside the terminal or app-based services (Uber/Ola).
Directions: Follow Airport Rd to Tonk Rd, then take JLN Marg to Sikar Rd (NH11). Continue past Chomu to reach the campus.
