IEMECON 2027

Call for Papers

Join us in advancing research through intelligent collaboration. Submit your original research to the 14th International Conference on Intelligence Embedded, MicroElectronics, Communication and Optical Networks.

UEM Jaipur
Feb 4-6, 2027
Jaipur, Rajasthan

The 14th International Conference on Intelligence Embedded, MicroElectronics, Communication and Optical Networks (IEMECON 2027) cordially invites researchers, scholars, scientists, engineers, industry professionals, and students to contribute their original research to this esteemed event. Hosted by the University of Engineering and Management, Jaipur, IEMECON 2027 stands as an energizing platform for exchanging knowledge and insights in the fields of Artificial Intelligence, Micro-electronics, Embedded, Communication, and Optical Networks.

Submission Guidelines

We welcome manuscripts that report on original, previously unpublished research and the latest developments within the conference's thematic areas. To ensure clarity and fairness, please adhere to the submission categories in the Technical Tracks.

Technical Tracks

  • Track-1: Next-Generation Smart Energy, Edge AI and Embedded Intelligence
  • Track-2: Microelectronics & VLSI
  • Track-3: Microwave, Photonics & Communication
