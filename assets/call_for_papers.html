IEMECON 2027

Call for Papers

Join us in advancing research through intelligent collaboration. Submit your original research to the 14th International Conference on Intelligence Embedded, MicroElectronics, Communication and Optical Networks.

UEM Jaipur
2nd -4th December, 2027
Jaipur, Rajasthan

Technical Tracks

  • Track-1: IoT & Embedded Systems
  • Track-2: Microelectronics & VLSI
  • Track-3: Advanced Materials & Nanotechnology
  • Track-4: Antennas, RF & Microwave Engineering
  • Track-5: Communication Systems & Optical Networking
  • Track-6: Power Electronics, Machines & Energy Systems
  • Track-7: Computational Intelligence, Software & Cybersecurity

IEEE Xplore Publication

All accepted and presented papers will be submitted for publication to the digital library of IEEE Xplore—a premier platform for peer-reviewed technical content.

Global reach to millions of researchers worldwide
Enhanced discoverability through indexed searches
Prestigious recognition in the research community
Publication in IEEE Xplore ensures maximum visibility for your research and global recognition.

Join Us at IEMECON 2027!

IEMECON 2027 offers a platform to share your research with a global community of experts, fostering innovation, collaboration, and advancements in the realms of Artificial Intelligence, Micro-electronics, Embedded, Communication, and Optical Networks.

We eagerly anticipate your valuable contributions and look forward to welcoming you to Jaipur.
