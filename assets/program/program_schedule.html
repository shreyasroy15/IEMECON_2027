Key milestones for the 14th International Conference (IEMECON 2027)

Conference Schedule Timeline

Stay updated with all important dates and submission deadlines for IEMECON 2027

20th October, 2026

Full Paper Submission (Last Date)

Final deadline for submitting your complete research papers. Ensure all papers are submitted with complete author information and abstract.

26th December, 2026

Acceptance Notification

Authors will be notified about the acceptance or rejection of their submitted papers based on the review process.

10th January, 2027

Camera Ready Paper Submission

Submit your final camera-ready papers incorporating the reviewer feedback in the specified format.

26th January, 2027

Registration Deadline

Complete your registration to attend the conference. Early bird registration may offer special discounts.

4th - 6th February, 2027

Conference Date

Join us for three days of cutting-edge research presentations, networking opportunities, and industry insights.
