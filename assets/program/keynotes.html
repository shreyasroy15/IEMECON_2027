IEMECON 2027

Keynote Speakers

Meet the global thought leaders and distinguished experts who will share their frontier research at IEMECON 2027.

Director, West Campus — NSUT

Prerna Gaur

Prof. Prerna Gaur is the Director of West Campus, NSUT, and holds leadership roles in IEEE Region 10 and India Council. She previously served as Director of East Campus and Head of Engineering Departments at NSUT. She holds a Ph.D. in AI and Control, with extensive teaching and industry experience. A prolific researcher, she has published over 180 papers, guided numerous students, and leads NSUT’s Technical Business Incubator.
Associate Professor, University of Perugia

Cristiano Tomassoni

Cristiano Tomassoni earned his Ph.D. in Electronics Engineering from the University of Perugia in 1999. He held visiting positions at the Technical University of Munich and Otto-von-Guericke University. Early work focused on enhancing electromagnetic simulation methods. Since 2001, he has been an Associate Professor at the University of Perugia, teaching electromagnetics and microwave system design.
Research Scientist, UC Davis

Ratheesh K. Meleppat

Dr. Ratheesh K. Meleppat holds a Ph.D. in optical imaging from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) Singapore, along with B.Tech and M.Tech degrees from Indian universities. Currently a Research Scientist at UC Davis, his research focuses on advanced ophthalmic imaging using technologies like OCT, SLO, and AO, combined with image processing and ML/AI.
Professor, NSUT

Harish Parthasarathy

Dr. Harish Parthasarathy received his B.Tech. from IIT Kanpur (1990) and Ph.D. from IIT Delhi (1994). Since 2000, he has been with Netaji Subhas University of Technology (formerly NSIT), where he is a Professor in Electronics and Communication Engineering. His research and teaching focus on Digital Signal Processing and Neural Networks.
Head, CV & AI — Cardiff Met

Sandeep Singh Sengar

Dr. Sandeep Singh Sengar is a Senior Lecturer and Head of Computer Vision and AI at Cardiff Metropolitan University, UK. He leads the Computer Vision/Image Processing cluster and represents the British Computer Society. Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Copenhagen.
Lecturer, Engineering Design

Moupali Chakraborty

Dr. Moupali Chakraborty is a lecturer in Engineering Design and Manufacturing, specializing in electronics and instrumentation systems. Her research focuses on sensor systems, flexible and printed electronics, sustainable electronics, and device characterization. She earned her PhD from IIT Kharagpur.
Sensor R&D Engineer, Flusso Limited

Mithlesh Kumar

Dr. Mithlesh Kumar is a Sensor R&D Engineer at Flusso Limited, specializing in sensor technology, microelectronics, and VLSI design. He began his professional journey as a Research Associate at IIT Hyderabad (2021–2022), followed by a role as an Advanced Electrical Design Engineer at Honeywell.
