IEMECON 2027

Conference Proceedings

Publication of accepted and presented papers at IEMECON 2027

IEEE Xplore Digital Library

All accepted & presented papers will be submitted for inclusion in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library

Overview

The papers accepted and presented at IEMECON 2027 will be published in the conference proceedings, which are indexed and archived in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library. This ensures maximum visibility and accessibility of your research to the global academic and professional community.

Global Reach

Accessible to millions of researchers worldwide through the IEEE Xplore platform.

Citation Impact

Widely cited publications that increase your research impact in the academic community.

Indexing

Ensures work is discoverable through major databases and academic search engines.

Recognition

Provides professional recognition and strengthens your academic research credentials.

Publication Timeline

Paper Presentation

Occurs during the conference dates (see Important Dates). Presentation is mandatory.

Camera-Ready Submission

Final version submitted after incorporating feedback from the review process.

IEEE Processing Period

Verifying compliance and preparing manuscripts for institutional indexing.

Publication in IEEE Xplore

Typically indexed and live within 2-3 months post-conference.

Author Responsibilities

  • At least one author must attend and present the paper during the conference sessions.
  • Submit the camera-ready version with all reviewer-suggested revisions incorporated.
  • Strictly adhere to IEEE copyright policies and publication requirements.
  • Provide necessary author permissions, declarations, and prompt responses to the team.
