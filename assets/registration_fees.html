Registration Process

Category Selection

Check registration dates and confirm your fee category based on membership and status.

Prepare Payment

Fee includes conference breakfast, lunch, kit and session access. (Travel & accommodation not included).

Execute Transfer

Make payment using bank transfer or UPI. Include your Paper ID in remarks: IEMECON_PAPERID.

Submit Form

Complete and submit the official registration form after successful payment.
