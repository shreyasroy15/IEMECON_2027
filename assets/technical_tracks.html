IEMECON 2027

Technical Tracks

Explore Cutting-Edge Research Domains

Research Areas & Topics

IEMECON 2027 presents three comprehensive technical tracks covering research in Intelligent Embedded Systems, Microelectronics, Communication Networks, and Photonics.

01

IoT & Embedded Systems

  • IoT architectures, protocols & security.
  • Industrial IoT (IIoT) and Industry 4.0/5.0.
  • Human-machine interaction in embedded systems
  • Cyber-physical systems integration with IoT
  • Sensor fusion and multi-sensor embedded systems
  • Wearable and implantable embedded devices
  • Real-time embedded systems & RTOS.
02

Microelectronics & VLSI

  • Low-power VLSI design techniques.
  • High-performance digital IC design.
  • VLSI for AI/ML accelerators (neural network hardware, TPUs, NPUs).
  • FinFET, Gate-All-Around (GAA), and nanosheet transistor design.
  • Neuromorphic and brain-inspired computing circuits.
  • Quantum and nano computing circuits.
  • RF and millimeter-wave IC design (5G/6G applications).
  • Hardware security, IP protection, and hardware Trojans.
  • VLSI for biomedical applications.
03

Advanced Materials & Nanotechnology

  • Nanomaterials for renewable energy (solar cells, fuel cells).
  • Nanotechnology in biomedical engineering and drug delivery.
  • Nanocomposites for aerospace and automotive applications.
  • Materials for MEMS/NEMS devices.
  • Nanomaterials for environmental remediation and sensing.
  • Thin films and surface engineering.
  • Semiconductor materials and quantum dots.
  • Sustainable and green nanomaterials.
04

Antennas, RF & Microwave Engineering

  • Millimeter-wave (mmWave) antennas for 5G/6G applications.
  • MIMO and massive MIMO antenna systems.
  • Metasurface and metamaterial-based antennas.
  • RF front-end design for 5G/6G transceivers.
  • RF energy harvesting.
  • Intelligent Reflecting Surfaces (IRS)/Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS).
  • Antennas for vehicular (V2X) communication.
  • Electromagnetic sensing and imaging systems.
05

Communication Systems & Optical Networking

  • Cognitive radio and spectrum sharing.
  • Underwater and molecular communication.
  • Software-defined optical network.
  • Silicon photonics for communication systems.
  • Software-defined networking (SDN) and network function virtualization (NFV).
  • Edge computing and fog computing architectures.
  • Internet of Things (IoT) communication protocols.
  • Quantum communication and quantum key distribution.
06

Power Electronics, Machines & Energy Systems

  • High-frequency and high-efficiency power converters.
  • Power electronics for renewable energy integration.
  • Wireless power transfer systems.
  • Hydrogen and fuel cell energy systems.
  • Solar PV systems and MPPT techniques.
  • Electric Vehicles & Transportation Electrification.
  • Smart Energy Systems & Control.
  • Green hydrogen production.
07

Computational Intelligence, Software & Cybersecurity

  • TinyML, model compression, and quantization.
  • Quantum machine learning.
  • Digital twins and neuromorphic computing.
  • LLM-based code generation, repair, and review.
  • MLOps and engineering of AI-based systems.
  • Zero-trust architectures.
  • Malware analysis and reverse engineering.
  • Open-source dependency and supply chain security.
  • Post-quantum cryptography.
  • DevSecOps and CI/CD pipelines.
