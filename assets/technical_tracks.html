IEMECON 2027

Technical Tracks

Explore Cutting-Edge Research Domains

Research Areas & Topics

IEMECON 2027 presents three comprehensive technical tracks covering research in Intelligent Embedded Systems, Microelectronics, Communication Networks, and Photonics.

01

Next-Generation Smart Energy, Edge AI and Embedded Intelligence

  • Distributed generation and grid integration
  • Grid stability, protection, and resilience
  • Energy storage systems (BESS, supercapacitors)
  • Energy harvesting techniques
  • Solar PV systems and optimization
  • EV charging infrastructure
  • Embedded systems for energy
  • IoT and sensor networks
  • AI/ML-based energy forecasting
  • Real-time embedded control
  • FPGA/SoC-based smart energy
  • Cybersecurity in smart grids
  • Deep Learning architectures
  • Parallel Computing for IIoT
  • TinyML and Edge AI
  • Neuromorphic computing
  • Model compression for edge
  • Drone and UAV intelligence
  • Human–machine interface
  • Embedded AI in healthcare
  • Wearable and biomedical devices
  • Signal Processing & Vision
  • Generative AI and LLMs
  • Quantum machine learning
  • AI for cybersecurity
  • Federated learning & privacy
02

Microelectronics & VLSI

  • CMOS analog and digital circuit design
  • Low-power and ultra-low-power VLSI
  • High-speed circuit techniques
  • Advanced MOSFET and FinFET
  • Beyond-CMOS Nanoelectronics
  • Semiconductor material engineering
  • Chiplets based heterogeneous designs
  • Nanoelectronics architectures
  • Analog and mixed-signal IC
  • RF and microwave IC design
  • Hardware security & cryptographic ICs
  • Biomedical and wearable IC
  • VLSI for IoT and edge devices
  • Memory, Computing & Processor Design
  • MEMS/NEMS/MOEMS Devices
  • Thermal-aware VLSI design
  • Optoelectronics Materials & Devices
  • Neuromorphic Engineering
  • Implantable devices VLSI
  • Spintronics and magnetic memory
  • Carbon nanotube (CNT) electronics
  • Biomedical Electronic Devices
03

Microwave, Photonics & Communication

  • RF power amplifiers and LNAs
  • Microwave passive components
  • Reconfigurable antennas
  • Microwave imaging and sensing
  • EMI/EMC analysis and mitigation
  • PLLs and frequency synthesizers
  • Microwave in Medical Science
  • Milimeter-wave & THz Antennas
  • MIMO & 5G Communication
  • Nanophotonics & Metamaterials
  • Optical Communication & Networks
  • Photonic Materials & Devices
  • Satellite & Space Communication
  • Quantum Information & Processing
  • RF energy harvesting systems
  • Terahertz communication systems
  • Optical sensors and biosensors
  • Fiber & Guided Wave Photonics
  • Lasers & Nonlinear Optics
  • Metamaterial Absorbers
  • THz Metamaterial Sensors
  • Artificial Magnetic Conductor

Submission Guidelines

Authors are encouraged to submit original research papers addressing any of the topics listed in the three technical tracks. All submissions will be reviewed by the Technical Program Committee and evaluated based on originality, quality, and relevance.
