Author Guidelines

Standardizing Excellence in Research Submission

Overview

We welcome authors to submit their technical papers for the 14th International Conference on Intelligent Embedded, MicroElectronics, Communication and Optical Networks (IEMECON 2027) using Microsoft’s Conference Management Toolkit (CMT). Please adhere to the guidelines outlined below to ensure a smooth submission process.

Paper Preparation

  • Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished research manuscripts on the conference topics.
  • Authors must ensure that the similarity index produced by plagiarism detection software does not surpass 15 percent.
  • Submissions must be in PDF format and follow the IEEE conference paper templates (two-column format only).
  • The number of pages should not exceed 6 (Six) A4 size pages.
  • There will be a double-blind review of the paper. Therefore, do not include the authors' names in the submitted paper.
  • The paper must include an abstract of about 200 words with a maximum of five keywords.
  • Authors of the accepted papers will be informed by email. Necessary revisions will be communicated to the corresponding author.
  • Authors must incorporate suggestions and send the revised camera-ready copy within the declared time limit.

Paper Submission

Authors are required to submit their papers through Microsoft CMT. The submission procedure is outlined below:

Accessing the Submission System

To submit your paper, access the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (Microsoft CMT) via the provided submission link:

Official Submission Portal https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/IEMECON2025

Log in to Microsoft CMT using your credentials and click the "Create New Submission" link.

Author Information

Provide accurate information, including names, affiliations, and email addresses. Ensure the correct order of authors for publication.

Paper Uploading

Fill in the title, abstract, and upload your paper in Word/PDF format as per the provided template. Assign appropriate keywords.

Review Process

Technical Program Chairs will evaluate papers based on technical suitability, scope, and plagiarism. After evaluation, qualifying submissions proceed to double-blind review by at least three experts for each paper. Authors will be notified of acceptance/rejection and any necessary revisions via Microsoft CMT.
