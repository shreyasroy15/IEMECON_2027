Sister Conferences

About Sister Conferences

The IEM-UEM group is well-known for its significant contribution to the academic and professional landscape through organizing a variety of prestigious conferences in fields like engineering, management, and computer science. These conferences serve as a platform for researchers, practitioners, educators, and students to share knowledge, exchange ideas, and discuss the latest developments in their respective areas.

The conferences organized by the IEM-UEM group are recognized for their:

  • High-quality research presentations
  • International collaboration
  • Comprehensive publications
  • Networking opportunities

Our Sister Conferences

CCWC

IEEE Annual Computing and Communication Workshop and Conference

A premier platform for presenting cutting-edge research in computing and communication technologies.

IEMCON

International Conference and Workshop on Computing and Communication

An international forum for researchers to discuss advancements in computing and communication fields.

UEMCON

IEEE Annual Ubiquitous Computing, Electronics & Mobile Communication

Focusing on ubiquitous computing, embedded systems, electronics, and mobile communication innovations.

ICHCSC

International Conference on Human-Centric Smart Computing

Dedicated to exploring human-centered approaches to smart computing and intelligent systems.

Diverse Conference Portfolio

Through its portfolio of well-established conferences, the IEM-UEM group continues to foster innovation, academic excellence, and professional development across multiple domains of engineering, management, and computer science.

IEMECON 2027

International Conference on IoT in Social, Mobile, Analytics and Cloud.

Hosted by UEM Jaipur.
