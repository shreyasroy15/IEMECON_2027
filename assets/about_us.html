IEMECON 2027

14th International Conference on Intelligent Embedded, MicroElectronics, Communication and Optical Networks

February 4th-6th, 2027 | Jaipur, Rajasthan, India

Welcome to IEMECON 2027

It is our great pleasure to welcome you to the vibrant and historic city of Jaipur, Rajasthan, for the 14th International Conference on Intelligent Embedded, MicroElectronics, Communication and Optical Networks (IEMECON 2027). Hosted by the University of Engineering and Management (UEM), Jaipur, this prestigious gathering bridges the domains of intelligent systems and sustainable innovation.

With the overarching theme "Advancements of Artificial Intelligence in Embedded Systems," we aim to foster global collaborations that drive the digital world of tomorrow.

Experience Jaipur

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan and a UNESCO World Heritage City, offers an extraordinary blend of tradition and modernity. From the majestic forts of Amer and Nahargarh, the astronomical marvel of Jantar Mantar, to the opulent City Palace, the city exudes architectural grandeur and regal history. Known for its handicrafts, gemstone markets, and vibrant bazaars, Jaipur is also home to a rich culinary legacy.

Heritage Tours

Iconic palaces and forts including Amer Fort, City Palace, and Nahargarh Fort

Art & Crafts

Local art exhibitions and authentic shopping experiences in vibrant bazaars

Cultural Evening

Rajasthan's traditional dance and music including Ghoomar, Kalbelia, and Kathputli puppet shows

Culinary Delights

Authentic Rajasthani cuisine: Dal Baati Churma, Laal Maas, and Ghewar desserts
