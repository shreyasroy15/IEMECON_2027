Welcome to IEMECON 2027

It is our great pleasure to welcome you to the vibrant and historic city of Jaipur, Rajasthan, for the 14th International Conference Intelligence Embedded, MicroElectronics, Communication and Optical Networks (IEMECON 2027). This prestigious annual conference, hosted by the University of Engineering and Management (UEM), Jaipur, will be held from 2nd -4th December, 2027, providing both in-person and virtual participation options.

With the overarching theme “Advancements of Artificial Intelligence in Embedded Systems, Microelectronics, Communication, and Optical Networks” IEMECON 2027 will serve as an international platform for the exchange of frontier research and developments that drive the digital and connected world of tomorrow.
